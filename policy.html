Privacy Policy — Frontline: Last Stand

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Developer: Arainex

Frontline: Last Stand is a single-player game. It does not have accounts, online gameplay, ads, analytics, or a developer-operated game server.

Information stored on your device

The game saves progress and settings—such as completed missions, total kills, technology upgrades, highest endless wave, and graphics quality—in the app’s local storage on your device. This information is not sent to the developer. It remains on your device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app.

Google Fonts

When an internet connection is available, the app may request font files from Google Fonts. That request may disclose your device’s IP address and standard connection information to Google so it can deliver the fonts. The game does not send your saved progress to Google. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Sharing and security

The developer does not collect or sell your saved game data and does not share it with other parties. Your game data is kept in the app’s storage on your device; no copy is kept on a developer-operated server.
